Student Success Score

How the score is built

indicator score = clip((value − worst) / (best − worst), 0, 1) × 100
pillar = Σ indicator weight × indicator score
Success Score = Σ pillar weight × pillar

Fixed anchors (not percentiles) keep the score comparable across semesters. 2nd-year students have no placement data, so that pillar's 15% is shared proportionally across the others. Bands: Thriving ≥ 70, On track 55-70, Needs attention 40-55, Critical < 40.
