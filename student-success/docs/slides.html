The problem

Colleges find out students are struggling only after the results are out

  • Marks sit in the SIS, attendance in a biometric export, activity in the LMS, test scores with the placement cell, and concerns in mentor notebooks.
  • Each system uses its own student ID format, so nobody sees the whole student.
  • Reports look backwards (CGPA, final placement %). By the time they show a problem, the semester is over.
  • Mentors need to know which of their 20 mentees to call this week, and why.
  • T&P cell needs to know who won't be placement-ready, early enough to train them.
  • HoDs need department patterns and a list of interventions they can track.

Approach

From silos to decisions in one reproducible pipeline

7 raw sources→Clean & reconcile IDs→Unified student view→Success Score→Risk models→Segments + actions→Dashboard

26,694 raw rows → 600 student records. 15 kinds of data-quality fix, each logged with its row count:

  • 4 different roll-number formats (24-cse-007, email, un-padded)
  • Duplicate SIS rows; 16 spellings of 5 department names
  • Biometric "attended > held" glitches; scores stored as "72%" text
  • Multiple placement attempts (latest kept); skipped surveys (flagged, not hidden)

Derived signals the raw systems don't have:

  • LMS activity trend (last 4 weeks vs first 4): the early-warning signal
  • Attendance trend and weakest-subject attendance against the 75% rule
  • SGPA trajectory across semesters
  • Concern tags mined from free-text mentor remarks (financial, health, commute…)

At-risk identification

Two models trained on real outcomes, every flag explained

0.96AUC, academic risk (CGPA < 6 or backlogs at graduation)
0.87AUC, placement risk (not placed in 6 months)
95%of held-out students with a poor outcome were flagged Medium or High
83%of those flags were right
  • Logistic regression: each factor's push on risk is exact, not approximated.
  • Features whose direction contradicts domain sense are dropped and the model refit, so explanations are always actionable.
  • Policy rule alongside the models: the 75% attendance rule for exam eligibility.

Next steps

From prototype to campus rollout

  • Connect live exports (SIS, LMS, biometric) on a nightly schedule. The pipeline already handles their formats.
  • Calibrate weights and anchors with the academic council, then re-validate on the college's own past batches.
  • Track interventions and outcomes, so the platform learns which actions work.
  • Fairness audit across departments and genders each retraining cycle.
  • Role-based access: mentors see their mentees, HoDs see their department.
  • Weekly digest to mentors: "3 of your mentees need a call this week."

All data in this prototype is synthetic and generated to mimic real campus systems, including their quality problems. No real student information is used.
